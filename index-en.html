I like to understand how things work. Then make them work better.

I am studying Cross-platform Application Development (DAM). I work with Python, SQL and Flask / PHP, and build dynamic interfaces with JavaScript, Canvas and SVG.

My interest in Full Stack development drives me to connect every part of an application: from data modelling — entities, relationships, views and indexes — to the experience of the person using it.

In my projects, I explore automation and local artificial intelligence: assistants integrated with Chatwoot, content generation and workflows with Python and Docker. I learn by developing solutions to specific problems.
